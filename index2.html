Microstructural Defects in Upcycled Composites

Role: Lead Researcher | Aug 2025 – June 2026

Problem: Investigate how microstructural defects in thermally-bound discontinuous composites (TBDCs) influence viscoelastic behavior compared to virgin Carbon Fiber Reinforced Polymers (CFRP).

Approach: Conducted experimental dynamic mechanical analysis using a TA Instruments DMA Q800 (0.01% strain). Executed temperature sweeps (35°C to 250°C at 3°C/min, 1.0 Hz) and isothermal stress relaxation tests at Tg (130°C for 10 minutes).

Result: Presented findings at the ASME Structures, Structural Dynamics, and Materials Conference (Long Beach, CA). *Full quantitative dataset to be updated post-publication.*

Airfoil Angle of Attack CFD Simulation

Computational Aerodynamics Study

Problem: Quantify the effect of an airfoil's Angle of Attack (AoA) on flow velocity and pressure fields while validating numerical drag results against analytical models.

Approach: Modeled domain interaction using ANSYS Fluent Boolean extraction. Simulated flow fields across three discrete AoA conditions (7°, 10°, and 15°) by altering relative domain flow vectors.

Result: Mapped pressure/velocity gradients highlighting stagnation point dynamics. Analytical verification uncovered drag coefficient anomalies under domain manipulation, demonstrating the vital importance of domain setup in solver accuracy.

Thermofluid Simulation & Verification

Problem: Model thermal distribution and fluid dynamics through a cold-plate heat exchanger and verify conservation equations numerical convergence.

Approach: Imported CAD from McMaster-Carr into ANSYS Fluent. Generated independent meshing for fluid domain and piping walls. Simulated water flow at 340K inlet temperature.

Result: Captured gradient fields across outer piping and symmetry planes. Verified mass flow and heat transfer rate equilibrium (net flux = 0), confirming total Conservation of Mass and Energy.
